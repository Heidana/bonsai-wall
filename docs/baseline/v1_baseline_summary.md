## structure_distance baseline (median pairwise, seeds 1-30)

From the IR: mean of (1 - IoU of height-normalised silhouettes built from strokes and pad extents) and the total-variation distance of the length-weighted primary-branch angle histogram (24 bins; primaries are live order-1 strokes).

`raw` is the spec's definition. V1 mirrors each tree left or right at random, so `raw` counts a mirrored copy of the same tree as different. `canonical` flips each tree so its silhouette mass lies on the +x side first (DEVIATIONS D8, approved) and is the variety test.

**Target cap.** The target is 1.5 x V1's canonical median, capped at the *ceiling*: the median canonical distance between this combo's trees and the trees of the other styles of the same species. Variety inside a style must not exceed the distance between styles, or trees stop reading as that style. If V1 is already at or above the ceiling the target is V1's own median (no regression).
